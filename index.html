Results, ranked by net-of-tax ending wealth

Net-of-tax wealth accounts for the fact that traditional dollars still owe ordinary income tax, and taxable-brokerage dollars still owe capital-gains tax — Roth, traditional, and taxable dollars are not worth the same amount, so summing them at face value would overstate traditional-heavy scenarios.
